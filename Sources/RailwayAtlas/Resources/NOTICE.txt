Rail geometry and station tags are community-contributed OpenStreetMap data.
They may be incomplete or out of date. A missing attribute means unknown.
Apple basemap tiles are not bundled or exported.

mainland-osm-mask.json: WGS84 display-coordinate mask derived from OpenStreetMap
relations 270056 (China), 913110 (Hong Kong), and 1867188 (Macao), downloaded
2026-11-04 from https://polygons.openstreetmap.fr/ (params=0, full precision).
The file retains separate Hong Kong/Macao polygons in the legacy "exclude"
field. The default China-region Apple-basemap policy unions these polygons;
the optional mainland-only policy subtracts them. Taiwan is outside this mask.
These are display-transform policies, not legal boundary interpretations.
OSM contributors, ODbL 1.0; the derived mask is distributed with the source.
The Hong Kong source was generated on 2026-10-31.

china-overview.railcache: 12 Z4 OpenRailwayMap tiles from the national viewport
viewed on 2026-11-03, with original source URLs and timestamps. OSM ODbL 1.0.
This small startup baseline does not include nationwide high-zoom detail.

Primary railway vector tiles: OpenRailwayMap
https://openrailwaymap.app/
https://github.com/hiddewie/OpenRailwayMap-vector/blob/master/USAGE.md

Independent fallback tiles:
VersaTiles (Shortbread / OpenStreetMap): https://versatiles.org/
OpenFreeMap / © OpenMapTiles / © OpenStreetMap contributors:
https://openfreemap.org/  https://openmaptiles.org/

Fallback tiles omit detailed railway attributes and low-zoom rail coverage.
No speed or ownership is inferred when absent. Cache exports preserve source
URLs, timestamps and OSM-derived raw vector data. They are ODbL databases, not
an Apple basemap export or a complete nationwide offline dataset.

Metro route relations: OpenStreetMap through VK Maps Overpass,
Private.coffee Overpass, or overpass-api.de.

Optional online colour metadata:
Wikidata P465 (CC0 structured data), https://www.wikidata.org/
Wikipedia explicit infobox colour fields, https://www.wikipedia.org/
Individual entity/article source links are retained with the cached colour.
Wikipedia content is subject to its applicable CC BY-SA terms; no articles or
images are redistributed by this app. Wiki colours are not labelled official.

Shanghai Metro line colours in the bundled Shanghai package use the published
official line marker palette from:
https://service.shmetro.com/shMetro2/metro.jsp
Other metro colours use the OSM route relation colour tag when present.

Bundled metro-palette.json: RMG Palette community colour data, 229 city/region
catalogues and 2784 entries, from railmapgen/rmg-palette revision
402c272b090c1548c5ffc867bb5d84469427278f:
https://github.com/railmapgen/rmg-palette
The original palette fields and names are retained. This data is independently
licensed under GPL-3.0; see METRO-PALETTE-LICENCE.txt included beside this notice.
Reproduce it with Scripts/build-metro-palette.mjs; the script records the pinned
source URLs and adds the local alias catalogue in Scripts/metro-palette-aliases.json.
No upstream application code is incorporated. Community colours are labelled
as community data, not verified operator standards. Entries may describe
planned or historical lines; the palette supplies no geometry or operating status.

included OSM object timestamp. Reproduce with Scripts/build-metro-snapshots.py
and pyosmium. These are fallback snapshots, not live coverage guarantees.
An online relation response replaces the corresponding snapshot tile.
